Frequently asked questions

What else you might want to know.

What does the initial consultation cost?

Nothing. The initial consultation is free and non-binding.

How fast do you respond?

Within 24 hours, often faster.

What happens if the position can't be filled?

We do everything we can to fill your position successfully. We can't guarantee a hire, though, and we discuss this transparently during the initial consultation.

Do I have to conduct the interviews myself?

Yes, the interviews are yours to run. We make sure you only talk to candidates who truly fit, and we keep working until the position is filled.

What happens after the initial consultation?

You'll know what your open position needs, and we agree on next steps together.
