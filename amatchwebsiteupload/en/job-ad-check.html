How it works

The check is fully automatic: you enter the link to your job ad, we evaluate the role description, application process, employer benefits and reach, and show you the result in about 10 seconds, right on screen, with first concrete pointers. If you'd like to go deeper, we're happy to discuss the results in a free initial consultation.
